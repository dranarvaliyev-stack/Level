    A quick guide

    Three steps to a good fit.

    1. 01

      Find your light.

      Face a window or a lamp so the camera can see you clearly, without a bright light behind you.

    2. 02

      Make room to move.

      Step back until your shoulders and hips are in frame, then turn, reach and walk as you normally would.

    3. 03

      Keep the look.

      Capture a still and it saves straight to your device, ready to compare or send to a friend.
